A gentle “shh” when voices get loud

Made for autistic kids who shout while using the tablet, and for the families who support them. When a scream is heard, a friendly character pops up and goes “shh”. No scolding, no scares: just a clear signal your child can see.

How it works

It always works the same way, so the reminder is predictable and never startling.

1

It listens

While the screen is on, it measures how loud the room is. It never records.

2

It notices a scream

If the sound stays loud for a moment, it counts as a scream. A slammed door or a quick bang doesn’t.

3

It reminds, gently

The screen dims softly, a character appears and a “shh” plays. Two seconds later, everything is back to normal.

You stay in control

Everything is set up from one simple screen, made for parents, caregivers and therapists.

Sensitivity that fits your child

Talk and watch the green bar move. Place the dot just above a normal voice, so only shouts reach the orange zone.

Follow the week

See how many reminders there were today and over the last seven days.

Their favorite characters

It comes with a girl and a boy, and you can add the characters your child already knows and loves (as PNG images). One appears at random with each reminder.

Your audio never leaves the device

Shusher doesn’t even have internet access. Sound is only used to measure the volume and is discarded right away. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
