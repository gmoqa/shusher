Shusher helps autistic kids (and any kid) notice when their voice gets too loud while they use a tablet or phone, without scolding and without scares.

When the app hears a scream, the screen dims softly and a friendly character appears going "shh", with a short sound. Two seconds later everything is back to normal, and your child can keep watching or playing.

HOW IT WORKS
• It listens in the background while the screen is on.
• If the sound stays in the orange zone for a moment, it counts as a scream. Short bangs, like a slammed door, don't count.
• The reminder appears over any app: videos, games, drawing apps. Touches outside the character still reach the app below.

DESIGNED NOT TO STARTLE
• Soft fade in and out, a night-blue backdrop and nothing that flashes.
• Always the same three steps, so the reminder is predictable.

FOR PARENTS, CAREGIVERS AND THERAPISTS
• Live sensitivity meter: talk and watch the green bar; place the dot just above a normal voice.
• Daily log with the last 7 days.
• Comes with a girl and a boy; add PNG images of the characters your child loves.
• Quick Settings button to turn it on and off from the notification shade.
• English, Spanish, Portuguese and French.

PRIVACY
Shusher has no internet access. Sound is only used to measure volume and is discarded right away; nothing is recorded or shared. The microphone rests while the screen is off.

Free and open source: github.com/gmoqa/shusher
